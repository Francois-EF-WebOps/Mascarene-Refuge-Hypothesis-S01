# KIT.md — Reproducible Research and Claim Audit Kit

**Purpose:** make one research claim traceable, contestable and reproducible without treating the hypothesis as an established conclusion.

## Read first
- `README.md`
- manuscript/preprint and methods notes
- source list, evidence ledger and version history, where present
- `LICENSE` and attribution/rights statements

## Requirements artefacts
For each claim under review record: claim ID, exact wording, claim type, source, precise page/section/archival reference, source date, quotation or faithful summary, evidence status, counter-evidence, what the source does **not** establish, and reviewer/date.

## Low-cost sourcing
Prioritise open primary sources, library/catalogue records and legally accessible scholarly material. Track access restrictions, reproduction rights and source quality. Do not fabricate citations or reproduce material without a lawful basis. Record unavailable sources as gaps, not as evidence.

## Procedure
1. Select one narrow claim and freeze its wording.
2. Identify what evidence would support, weaken or falsify it.
3. Retrieve the source and record precise location plus bibliographic metadata.
4. Separate source text from interpretation and hypothesis.
5. Search for credible counter-evidence and alternative explanations.
6. Update the claim ledger and manuscript only where evidence justifies it.
7. Have a second reader attempt to reproduce the claim from the recorded source.
8. Record unresolved uncertainty and the next research action.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Claim has a stable ID and bounded wording.
- [ ] Source and precise location are retrievable.
- [ ] Supporting and contrary evidence are considered.
- [ ] Limitations and non-establishing points are explicit.
- [ ] Licence/attribution is respected.
- [ ] Another reader can reproduce the reasoning.
- [ ] Hypothesis, interpretation and established fact remain distinct.

**Deliverable:** one claim-audit record plus a short reproducibility note; no claim of historical proof without adequate evidence.
